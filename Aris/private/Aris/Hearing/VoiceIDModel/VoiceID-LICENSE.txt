ArisVoiceFBank.mlmodelc and ArisVoiceEmbedding.mlmodelc (Aris's on-device speaker embedding, F1 "the owner's voice")

These are FBank.mlmodelc and Embedding.mlmodelc of FluidInference/speaker-diarization-coreml
(https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/speaker-diarization-coreml, artifact snapshot
1ed7a662fdc7109e36d822db793ee6eebdaf8594), unchanged except for their folder names. weight.bin SHA-256:
  Embedding 99356b2985b8d43880a657024d941d450b38820451ccff903f76ed4e52d1868b
  FBank     9e83fdd3ea78064b078069e4d9141603c61c47a27fd19e7e3142ff7476f8db36

They are Core ML conversions by Fluid Inference of the speaker embedding of pyannote's
speaker-diarization-community-1 (https://huggingface.co/pyannote/speaker-diarization-community-1), a WeSpeaker
ResNet34 (Wang et al., "Wespeaker: A research and production oriented speaker embedding learning toolkit",
ICASSP 2023). Fluid Inference converted the PyTorch components to Core ML, introduced fixed and enumerated input
shapes, applied mixed-precision storage, and separated the FBank frontend from the embedding backend.
